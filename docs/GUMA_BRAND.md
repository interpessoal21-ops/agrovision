# GUMA — Brand system

## Brand
**GUMA** is an Earth-intelligence platform connecting climate, Earth observation, agricultural systems, territorial analysis and collaborative research.

The name is inspired by Guma, the character in Jorge Amado's *Mar Morto*. The reference evokes the sea, navigation, courage and the horizon. This is an independent cultural inspiration; GUMA is not affiliated with or endorsed by the author, rights holders or publisher.

## Positioning
**Earth intelligence. Beyond agriculture.**

GUMA turns Earth-observation and environmental datasets into transparent, traceable and interpretable evidence for researchers, students, agricultural professionals, institutions and public decision-makers.

## Logo concept
The mark combines an orbital path, a horizon/wave and a point of navigation. It represents observation, movement and the relationship between ocean, atmosphere and land. Use the standalone mark at small sizes and the wordmark with the descriptor **EARTH INTELLIGENCE** in primary placements.

## Typography
Use Inter or Manrope where available, with system sans-serif fallbacks. Prefer strong, compact headings and highly legible data labels.

## Interface principles
1. Scientific provenance is visible: source, period, spatial scope, update time and quality.
2. Separate observations, derived indicators, scenarios and interpretations.
3. Do not present screening tools as calibrated forecasts or agronomic prescriptions.
4. Keep maps and data visualizations visually calm; use lime and amber sparingly for emphasis.
5. Support Portuguese and English without changing the scientific meaning.

## Voice
Clear, curious, rigorous and accessible. Avoid inflated claims such as “real-time” when source publication is delayed, or “AI-powered” when a feature is not actually AI-based.
