# GUMA — Partnerships & Alliances Roadmap

**Status:** prospecting framework; no organization listed here is a confirmed GUMA partner.

## Purpose

Build a credible ecosystem around GUMA through public data access, scientific review, field validation, geospatial infrastructure, applied research, funding routes and responsible adoption.

## Priority opportunity map

| Organization / initiative | Potential contribution | GUMA's possible contribution | First step |
|---|---|---|---|
| INPE | Earth-observation products, land-cover, fire and deforestation monitoring | Reproducible integrations, source attribution and use cases | Review public data products and identify a technical contact |
| INMET | Meteorological station observations and AgroMet context | Station-vs-grid validation notebooks and dashboards | Select a pilot territory and document station metadata/access |

| FINEP / COOPERAMAIS | Potential funding for eligible R&D proposals | Defined technical work package and measurable deliverables | Check call rules, applicant eligibility and consortium requirements |

## Partnership sequence

1. Publish a one-page technical brief, architecture diagram, live demo and clear limitations.
2. Create a source register containing provider, license, API, spatial resolution, temporal coverage, update cadence and known uncertainty.
3. Seek independent review from a university or research group.
4. Run a narrow pilot comparing NASA POWER gridded data with nearby INMET station observations for one territory and a documented period.
5. Only then approach applied R&D and funding programs with an eligible institution, company, research unit, cooperative or consortium as required.
6. Formalize data licensing, attribution, privacy, intellectual property, publication rights, maintenance and responsibilities before scaling.

## Earth systems expansion

The platform's long-term scope can extend beyond agriculture:

- **Oceans and cryosphere:** sea level, ocean heat, sea-surface conditions, glaciers and ice sheets.
- **Forests, biodiversity and fire:** native vegetation, restoration, habitat context, fire scars and ecosystem change.
- **Land use and territorial change:** land-cover transitions, agriculture, pasture, mining and urban expansion.
- **Water and environmental risk:** river basins, water availability, floods, droughts, landslides and observation networks.

These are roadmap areas, not claims that the datasets are already integrated. Each layer must pass source, license, provenance, spatial/temporal resolution and quality checks before being presented as operational evidence.

## Governance rules

- A prospect is not a partner. Display a relationship as confirmed only after explicit written agreement.
- Never imply endorsement by a public institution or private company.
- Check eligibility, current calls, licensing and terms before applying or integrating data.
- Keep public data attribution and source links visible.
- Do not exchange personal, farm-level or confidential data without a documented legal basis and consent.
- Publish methods, validation results, uncertainty and limitations alongside outputs.
